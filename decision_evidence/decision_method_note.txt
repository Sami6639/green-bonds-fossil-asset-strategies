Decision-chain evidence: scope and method
10 October 2026

Purpose and selection
This bounded extension examines how selected publicly documented asset decisions accompany continuing green-bond assignments. It preserves the financing–fossil-asset research axis and does not attempt causal process identification. Events were selected retrospectively for substantive importance and relevance to the existing three-core-case archive: Enel’s Australian/Hellas ownership restructuring and allocation replacement; Iberdrola’s Mexico sale; and SSE’s Triton acquisition and Keadby 2 investment/commissioning. Tarbert was added as a small same-question check because it is the physical closure inside SSE’s matched FY2024 interval. The choice was event-informed, not prospective or exhaustive; it cannot identify the prevalence of a strategy among utilities.

Common questions
Each chain records: (1) dated public decision; (2) declared strategic rationale and whether contemporaneous or retrospective; (3) disclosed conditions; (4) disclosed alternatives or the absence of an observed formal comparison; (5) achieved transaction/operating milestone; (6) physical versus ownership outcome; (7) corporate capital-allocation rationale; (8) direct linkage to the selected financing instrument; and (9) unresolved fields and window role. The long matrix applies every field to every event. Statements about expected returns, future emissions, conversion technologies or strategic purpose are coded as issuer/partner claims; they are not independent findings about true motivation or realized effects.

Search boundary and stop rule
The search was restricted to the chosen events, their announcement/completion records and directly relevant official strategy presentations, instrument frameworks/allocation records and annual-report entries. Issuer materials were preferred for the announcement and assignment they themselves make. Equinor and Siemens were used only as transaction/construction-partner corroboration, not as independent environmental evaluators. Search and access occurred on 10 October 2026. Historical observations remain bounded to the manuscript’s 2019–2024 windows; the 2018 Keadby decision is explicit antecedent context. A current undated Tarbert site description is only retrospective corroboration. Iberdrola’s later 2025–2026 sale is outside scope and is not evidence for the 2024 event.

The stopping condition was an accessible announcement/decision and implementation record for each selected event, paired with the applicable instrument-boundary evidence, and an explicit outcome for every common field. A source that did not reveal a financing link produced a corpus-bounded nonfinding; it did not trigger an open-ended search through new issuers or later transactions. No interviews, internal board papers, full contracts, private cash records, dispatch data or exhaustive legal audit were available. The evidence therefore identifies reported rationales and observable outcomes, not causal necessity, confidential alternatives, managerial intent or all relevant cash flows.

Link coding
SOURCE-EXPLICIT INSTRUMENT LINK: A source expressly connects an ownership/reporting change to an allocation action. Enel’s report names deconsolidation as the reason for the selected replacements. The contemporaneous transactions and annual ownership records establish related restructuring, but the exact allocation-effective days are unknown. Prior held-for-sale/debt accounting dates must not be silently equated with the transaction closing dates.
CORPORATE LINK, INSTRUMENT LINK NOT DOCUMENTED: The issuer expressly presents capital recycling or functional complementarity, but the inspected records do not connect the asset decision or its receipts/expenditure to the selected bond. This applies to Iberdrola’s sale rationale and SSE’s gas decisions. It neither proves no economic connection exists nor permits a bond-funded fossil investment claim.
REPORTED REGULATORY CONSTRAINT, INSTRUMENT LINK NOT DOCUMENTED: Tarbert’s closure is explained by the issuer through its environmental licence; no GB5 closure requirement is identified. The underlying licence was not independently inspected.

Ownership and physical boundaries
Iberdrola’s buyer-trust PPAs and temporary management-services contract establish disclosed contractual connections after sale. They do not imply retained equity. The annual report’s planned purchases from four sold stations are forward-looking contract language, not observed deliveries or proof all transferred assets continued producing. Its transaction perimeter of 8,539 MW (8,436 gas plus 103 wind) is separate from the operating-table 8,494 MW gas reduction; the 58 MW mismatch remains unresolved. Enel’s joint control is subsidiary-level and is not a direct claim about every plant-level stake. Triton is an existing-asset acquisition; Keadby 2 is new gas commissioning; prospective hydrogen/CCS conversion is not an achieved outcome. Tarbert’s old oil station is separate from later proposed site uses.

Timing and unresolved observations
Enel and Iberdrola decisions are nested in their six-vintage histories. Triton closing (1 September 2022) and Keadby 2 commissioning (15 March 2023) precede the 31 March 2023 opening of SSE’s matched allocation/maturity interval; they are prior context, not simultaneous within-window events. Tarbert is inside that interval. Its 2024 prospectus reports closure in December in the narrative and September in a footnote; exact month is unresolved, while FY2024 inclusion and the 620 MW closure are unaffected. No artificial daily lag is calculated for annual or monthly forecasts. The optional target–realization file contains only explicitly dated, matched public statements; it is not a comprehensive implementation-performance score.

Access, verification and files
Decision_source_register.csv identifies official URLs, dates, locators, access depth and new versus reused evidence. Decision_source_claims.csv contains paraphrased claim-level evidence with boundaries. Decision_chain_matrix.csv contains common-field comparisons. Table3_decision_chain_synthesis.csv offers four concise manuscript display columns plus source/timing metadata; its note belongs with the table. Existing allocation histories and capacity bridges were reused rather than reconstructed in this research pass. Iberdrola’s strategy p4 and annual-report printed p320/PDF p329 were visually checked; the latter page offset was verified directly because offsets vary inside the report. Enel agreement PDFs and relevant archived annual/green-report text were inspected. The SSE 2018 script was available as official PDF web text; a direct download returned HTTP403 and screenshot retrieval failed, so visual PDF verification is not claimed. No independent human double coding or complete gross-event audit is claimed.

Only derived CSVs, this method note, optional target comparisons and manifests belong in the reproducible data package. Local_sources and downloaded/rendered source material are research aids and must not be redistributed as original reports in the derived package. The register retains public URLs rather than requiring copyrighted source copies for provenance. The download manifest accurately distinguishes successful downloads from the SSE download failure.
