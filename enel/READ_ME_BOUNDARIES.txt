Enel eligibility and history pilot | retrieved 10 October 2026

Eligibility: passes the green use-of-proceeds instrument and incumbent-fossil screens. Enel Finance International NV is controlled by Enel SpA; the 2017/2018/2019 green bonds are guaranteed by Enel SpA and allocate to named renewable assets. The 2024 green-bond report p4 documents EUR2.25bn outstanding: 2018 EUR1.25bn (renewables plus grids), 2019 EUR1bn (renewables only). The 2023 report p4 documents the full three-bond history. Later sustainability-linked bonds are separate general-purpose instruments; they must not be added to UoP allocations. ISIN crosswalk and maturity explanation for disappearance of 2017 reporting in2024 remain unverified here.

Key boundaries
1. Consolidation:2019 report PDFp248 and2023p370 define line-by-line group scope, with a proportionately consolidated Spanish nuclear joint operation and explicitly noted equity-method exceptions. Do not call the panel equity-share capacity or globally retired capacity.2019 Reftinskaya divestment,2022 Russia exit,2023 Argentine thermal sales and2024 Peru sales change the corporate perimeter.
2. Closure versus sale: Reftinskaya sale removes capacity/generation from consolidation while some licenses/personnel remained temporarily. It is not retirement. BocaminaII permanent disconnection in2022 and Fusina/AsPontes removal in2023 provide distinct physical-event evidence. Event quantities are not sufficiently complete to attribute the entire six-year decline.
3. Utilization:2024 coal capacity stays4627MW, generation falls10755→2377GWh. Annual report p177 relates the high2023 Italian coal dispatch to preventive gas-use measures.2022 sustainabilityp151 documents drought/geopolitics and delayed closure authorizations. These are documented rival explanations to renewable displacement.
4. Allocation:2019 bond initially986m rounded, of which most is additional capitalized expenditure on projects also listed under2017/2018 issues. The source says finance/refinance; financial labels cannot establish counterfactual additionality. Do not double-count cross-issue plant MW.
5. Replacements:2020 report PDFp13 footnote1 says Girgarre awaits reauthorization and roughlyEUR7m moved toAmistadIV.2023 reportp4 reallocatesEUR229.60m after three deconsolidations. Follow original and current portfolios separately; sale is not project disappearance.
6. Reporting:2019/2020 ESG tables replace actual output with expected output for certain late entrants. Missing dashes are not zero.2024 reporting changes coverage and cumulative values do not simply equal prior cumulative plus annual output. Retain the raw values until scope/period/restatement is reconciled; no automatic correction. Italian hydro is aggregated repowering; the2024 33MW/1567GWh must not be interpreted as output of33MW of new capacity. Earlier reports say incremental repowering output cannot be separated.
7. Rounding:2023 source renewable total55536MW versus2024 comparative55537MW, and generation126985 versus126986GWh. Fuel components and totals can differ by1. These are retained with source-vintage labels rather than silently standardized.

Scientific status: issuer-level descriptive history and instrument eligibility are supported. Full project-financing-to-delivery and physical-retirement branches remain conditional on complete annual cohort extraction, historical ownership shares, event-level MW reconciliation and post-sale asset tracing. No causal financing effect, fixed-boundary decline, or completed exhaustive case dossier is claimed.
