Figure 1. Source-defined capacity directions across 37 utility-group windows: 33 principal fuel-identified scopes and 4 financing-matched thermal-proxy extensions. Groups are ordered alphabetically. Triangles indicate increases or decreases; circles denote no change at reported precision, and crosses identify unresolved single-boundary comparisons. Dates are month-end cutoffs. ¹ Scope and basis labels are abbreviated; complete ownership, technology and operating-scope qualifications are retained in source-linked figure data and supplementary tables. ² Filled fossil symbols represent principal source-defined fossil series or identified component subsets; hollow symbols identify unresolved thermal-proxy mixtures, which are excluded from principal fossil counts. Filled symbols do not certify an exhaustive active-plant census. Iberdrola retains coal plus gas, excluding unspecified cogeneration; J-POWER and Statkraft show coal-capable and gas-only components, respectively. ³ Filled RE symbols identify storage-excluded renewables; hollow symbols retain broader, storage-inclusive or insufficiently verified reported categories. The storage-exclusion check does not establish a pure-fossil comparator. Period lengths, reporting boundaries and financing timing differ, so arrows are unweighted within-group descriptions, not comparable rates, pooled MW totals, causal effects or performance ranks.

Figure 2. Descriptive sensitivity of paired capacity directions to retrospective materiality bands. Bars count utility groups at 0%, 0.5% and 1% of each series’ opening capacity; an absolute endpoint change less than or equal to the stated band is within band. At 0%, this denotes no change at source precision. Denominators require resolved same-window observations for both series: principal fuel-identified scopes n = 32, broader series including thermal proxies n = 36, principal scopes: storage-excluded re n = 8, principal scopes: wind + solar n = 11. Panel A draws from 33 principal fuel-identified scopes; panel B includes all 37 broader matched groups, including thermal proxies. Panels C and D intersect the principal cohort with storage-excluded renewables and separately observed wind-plus-solar components, respectively; panel D is not a total-renewable measure. A clean renewable boundary does not re-admit unresolved thermal proxies into panels C or D. The fossil comparator remains source-defined and scope-qualified throughout. Each group contributes at most once per bar. “Other resolved pairs” includes all pairs without a renewable-series increase at the stated band. Missing or incompatible comparisons are excluded rather than treated as zero. Bar lengths are normalized to each paired denominator and integers display unweighted counts, not pooled capacity, population prevalence estimates, statistical tests or causal effects.

Figure 3. Allocated financial stock associated with achieved full operation at exact reporting cutoffs. Each percentage equals the sum of allocated financial amounts attached to reporting entries that had achieved full operation by the cutoff, divided by the instrument’s allocated financial stock at that cutoff. Panel A retains the same three SSE GB5 projects and £543.9 million allocation stock at 31 March 2023 and 31 March 2024: the full-operation share increases from 6.84% to 69.48% as Seagreen reaches full operation. Allocation amounts and weights remain fixed; the increase is not a new annual financing flow. Panel B uses the two persistent reporting entries of Wisconsin Electric Power Company’s September 2022 green bond, within WEC Energy Group. Allocated stock rises from $281.7 million at 30 June 2023 to $440.5 million at 30 June 2024, with cutoff-specific weights; the full-operation share increases from 0.00% to 33.46% after Badger Hollow II enters service. Paris is a bundled solar-and-battery reporting entry. The not-yet-full-operation category can include construction, partial operation and generation; a zero indicator does not assert zero production. These are instrument-specific stage associations, not measured generation shares, avoided emissions, comparable project-performance scores or identified financing effects.
