IBERDROLA EMPIRICAL PILOT, 2022–2024
Compiled 10 October 2026 from official issuer reports. Descriptive measurement pilot, not a balanced causal panel.

Coverage and data dictionary
- issuer_year_technology_facts.csv: 216 numeric facts, 3 years × 2 metrics × 12 reported technology/subtotal rows × 3 ownership scopes. Each value includes report vintage, exact official URL, 1-based physical PDF page, printed page, table, row and field.
- Preserve scopes separately: consolidated at EBITDA level; proportional managed investees; and published total combining these. Capacity and generation share the chosen column boundary. Total-group is not gross 100% physical capacity, and consolidated is not equity-net ownership.
- 2023 and 2024 use the latest 2024 report;2022 is the comparative in 2023. An older 2023 generation breakdown differs by 240 GWh consolidated/investee cogeneration, while total-group generation is unchanged. See comparative_revision_audit.csv. No full retrospective constant-ownership perimeter is claimed.
- Primary components are onshore/offshore wind, hydro/mini-hydro, solar/other, reported Batteries, nuclear, CCGT, cogeneration. Renewables/Thermal are subtotals, and Total Group is a total: never add all rows. Thermal includes nuclear; no coal zero is inferred from a missing row. The reported Batteries category includes an apparent fuel-cell ambiguity. Solar capacity is MWdc.
- green_bond_instrument_facts.csv:116 facts. Complete 2024 holding issuance cohort is 8 ISINs from 6 transactions; no Avangrid/Neoenergia-issued instruments or other loan cohorts included. Separate matched March 2022 bond continuity case covers 2022–2024 and must not be counted as 2024 issuance.
- green_bond_project_allocations.csv:36 rows for the complete 2024 cohort and 9 time-specific rows for the matched bond. Repeated project names across tranches and assignment intervals are intentional. Project-level money and actual generation are unavailable in these allocation tables and remain empty; totals exist only at instrument/area level.
- Financial allocation is a reported assignment of investment to an instrument, including future-designated spending. It is neither an annual new-capex flow nor proof of expenditure. Original currencies and year-end EUR equivalents are separate from issuance-FX EUR allocations. Do not sum allocations across report years as new financing.
- Startup years later than the report year are expectations. Historical/current years are the issuer's reported startup years, not independently verified final commercial-operation dates. Attributable capacity is not full project capacity and may refer to unbuilt assets. Instrument output is actual reported financing-attributed output for the assignment period, not a project forecast or incremental causal effect.

Primary transition finding and bridge
Consolidated global CCGT capacity changes from 16,131 MW at end 2023 to 7,654 MW at end 2024, a net decline of 8,477 MW. The operating tables report 8,494 MW disposed in Mexico; the remaining+17 MW is explicitly attributed to Termopernambuco modernisation in Brazil. Thus 16,131−8,494+17=7,654 MW, with unexplained net residual 0 MW after the two documented events. See ccgt_capacity_bridge_2023_2024.csv. Zero net residual does not establish absence of offsetting unreported gross changes.
The Mexican transaction completed 26 February 2024 (annual 2024 printed 171/PDF 182). This date is sale completion, not physical plant closure. The deal's 8,539 MW combined-cycle-plus-wind headline differs from the operating tables'8,494 MW CCGT plus 103 MW wind decline. The 58 MW cross-disclosure difference is retained as unresolved; the primary CCGT bridge consistently uses the operational series.

Quality and interpretation
The green-finance report excludes Avangrid/Neoenergia-issued transactions while global generation includes their assets. Consequently this cohort cannot proxy whole-group green finance. No financing-caused retirement or emission reduction is inferred.
Twelve source-quality/interpretation findings are in quality_issues.csv, including summary-table category errors, inconsistent CHF EUR summaries, future assets labelled installed, a duplicate erroneous ISIN heading in 2022, within-year refinancing reassignment and ownership reclassification.
The same March 2022 bond changes its Baltic Eagle allocation after a 49% stake sale on 2 November 2023; the old and new rows are sequential, not additive. Its stated output is 0,19 and 321 GWh in 2022,2023 and 2024 respectively, but the asset basket changes.

Validation
build_pilot.py reproduces the CSVs from explicit audited transcriptions.132 arithmetic checks reconcile issuer component sums and ownership columns and the 2024 project capacity aggregates. The only nonzero arithmetic difference is 514 MW summed rounded January-hybrid project rows versus 512 MW stated instrument total. Values were retained unchanged. Primary-key uniqueness and required provenance checks pass. Selected table/footnote pages were visually inspected; page-specific evidence and QA images are retained. No external communication, login, payment or form submission was used.

Files
source_manifest.csv contains SHA-256 fingerprints and exact URLs for 5 downloaded PDFs. ownership_events.csv records disposal/upgrade timing and quantities. descriptive_metrics.csv contains transparent ratios only, with the same ownership scope in numerator/denominator. Source PDFs and page excerpts are research evidence; the CSVs and this note are the extracted deliverable.
