UTILITY TRANSITION PILOT
Snapshot: 10 October 2026

Scope
Four incumbent issuer groups are screened for 2022–2024. Project coverage differs by issuer. This is a data-quality and comparative accounting pilot, not a globally representative panel or a completed full study.

Start with analytical_coverage.csv. issuer_year_observations.csv standardizes column names only. It does not harmonize ownership, generation accounting or fuel definitions. Source tables retain separate units, currencies, fiscal periods, scopes, subtotals, allocation stocks/flows, actual/expected delivery and missing values.

Three narrower capacity comparisons are source-reconciled:
- Iberdrola: consolidated reported gas-fired capacity bucket (including OCGT), Mexico sale and Brazilian uprating
- SSE: reported Thermal capacity and the Tarbert oil-station closure
- CLP: equity coal capacity, physical closures and ownership exits

A zero net residual does not establish that unreported, offsetting gross changes are absent. No bond-caused closure, measured emissions reduction, or constant-perimeter generation effect is claimed. Ørsted's overlapping fuel-capability and standby categories remain unresolved.

Reproducibility
Issuer folders contain extracted facts, exact source locators, source manifests, audit results and Python transcription/build scripts. table_catalog.csv fingerprints the extracted CSV versions. Full source PDFs are excluded from the data-only archive because open redistribution rights have not been established. Some sources were accessible only as official web-indexed text; issuer notes disclose visual-verification limits.

Interpretation
Do not sum alternative ownership scopes, subtotals, repeated project outputs, successive annual snapshots of cumulative allocations, or overlapping fuel-capable units. A blank is not a zero. Refinancing is a financial classification and does not establish that the financed asset was already operating.

field_completeness.csv reports syntactic coverage, not analytical eligibility. Allocation-value coverage uses explicit cohorts and currencies. No global coverage percentage is manufactured, and complete whole-company green-finance coverage has not been established.

The 2019–2024 historical capacity/event evidence is in integrated_history. CLP closures are reserve-inclusive; Iberdrola historical technology capacity includes installed pre-COD and lease-ambiguous assets. This pilot contains no inferential regressions.
