HISTORICAL CAPACITY AND FINANCE EVIDENCE

This extension preserves four distinct issuer reporting boundaries. It is not a balanced, harmonized whole-company fossil panel. Use historical_capacity_cells.csv and historical_capacity_events.csv with the issuer-level bridges and notes.

Iberdrola uses its reported group capacity: consolidated at EBITDA level plus proportional managed investees. Its gas bucket includes OCGT, and cogeneration is a broad technology proxy whose fuel mix is not fully established. The original label is retained. Topolobampo was physically installed before commercial operation; Australian assets include unresolved ownership or lease status. This is a reported accounting/technology stock, not a core operating fossil stock.

SSE uses its reported Thermal portfolio, with owned and joint-venture capacity. Multifuel, transfers, scope changes and acquisition effects are separate. Fiscal years end 31 March.

CLP uses equity-share capacity. The coal event analysis is separate from its gas and other capacity. A late-2020 Mount Piper uprating and 2021 stock recognition mismatch is retained; annual bridges are not forced. The 2024 closure total includes 245 equity MW already in reserve from 2022; it is reserve-inclusive. Identified zero event contributions do not verify the absence of gross offsetting events.

The selected matched financial cohorts are illustrative longitudinal instruments, not full issuer finance universes. Iberdrola output is financing-attributed to a two-asset basket; CLP WENT output is full-project. They must not be pooled or relabelled as comparable additional generation. SSE links and dated project snapshots are retained separately; disclosure year does not imply a common observation date.

Expected versus actual output, allocation stocks versus annualflows, and sale/handback versus physical retirement remain distinct. No bond-caused transition estimate or whole-company comparable fossil GWh is constructed. Enel regional coal bridges and event dossiers remain separate: gross plant ratings, observed regional net changes and known disposal contributions are not interchangeable. Reftinskaya exited during 2019 and is outside the end-2019 to end-2024 bridge.
