Assessment rules
Supported means the exact bounded proposition is established by the available evidence. Contradicted means observed evidence directly conflicts with that proposition. Unidentified means necessary evidence is missing; it is not a negative finding. The comparison does not assume what investors normally believe, what outcomes a normal issuer should achieve, or any unnamed strategic mechanism.

Scope
The screen is retrospective, purposive and disclosure-conditioned. Core roles reflect selected analytical contrasts rather than a uniform completeness gate; Enel also lacks an exhaustive event inventory. EDF and ENGIE remain eligible additional candidates with partial extraction. Current CSVs supersede obsolete extraction-depth wording but do not change the archived audit. Enel has no missing row in the selected six financial tables; source-level hydro identity aggregation and partial generation coverage remain. SSE has a six-year physical/disclosure chronology but only two annual GB5 money snapshots. Iberdrola has six named-project snapshots but no project-specific money. EDF has six-year capacity, not a completed fourth core.

The enel_ownership_allocation_event_chain.csv supplement preserves subsidiary-level transactions, retained joint control, and the allocation replacement separately; commitment_source_ledger.csv preserves the originating source review.

The main tables summarize the full screen and matrix; they should be accompanied by their bounded case/instrument dates. All amounts are source currency allocation stocks unless stated otherwise. The explanatory propositions in Table 2 are analytical tests, not quotations of actual issuer promises or evidence of investor beliefs. No causal financing test is claimed.
