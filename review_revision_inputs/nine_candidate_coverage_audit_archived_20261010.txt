Nine-candidate evidence and eligibility audit
10 October 2026 | Public sources inspected, not a completed nine-firm panel

Selection disclosure
This is a retrospective, exploratory, purposive screen. Several2024 examples were known before the candidate frame was fixed. The candidate list is not a preregistered random sample, and the availability of detailed disclosure affects selection. Eligibility, analytical branch and extraction depth are distinct. An unextracted firm is not ineligible. No case is excluded because its outcome is inconvenient.

Recommended bounded next decision
Three deepest focal histories now: Iberdrola, SSE, Enel. Add EDF as the first new core case if named-project actual-status and historical capacity bridges can be completed. ENGIE is next, not disqualified; grouped monetary allocation may permit a transparent group-linked design. Ørsted and CLP remain substantive complementary boundary cases unless their unresolved core definitions can be resolved. EDP/RWE remain in the full nine-row coverage audit and can support a portfolio-level comparison, but cannot be made named-project cases by assuming links.

Do not require exactly five cases. A four-core/two-complementary structure can be scientifically clearer than a forced five-company pooled dataset. Capacity outcomes must be described at their actual fuel/segment/group boundaries. Number of countries or companies does not establish global representativeness.

Minimum missing evidence before full-article commitment
For each central implementation claim: a qualifying bond/project or explicitly grouped linkage, a dated prior strategic decision, a physical or ownership event withMW and basis, and sufficient post-event evidence to distinguish a completed action from an announcement. For an overall capacity-decline attribution: complete component coverage or honest partial-attribution bounds. For sold-asset continuation: evidence from the later owner/operator. For a full financing-to-delivery claim: actual commissioning/operation independent of an unverified expected status. A complete annual project panel is necessary only for annual project estimates; selected episodes cannot be labeled complete-cohort outcomes.

Scientific stopping gate
After the next extraction cycle, decide claim by claim, not by a quality score. Continue to a BSE-oriented full comparative article only if the evidence establishes an analytically useful contrast between financed project delivery and physically versus ownership-mediated portfolio change, beyond examples already shown by Alova/Pichler and green-bond studies. If the evidence amounts to recent anecdotes plus general corporate narratives, deliver a reproducible pilot/research note and identify the unresolved research requirement. More polished prose cannot turn that into a tested strategic mechanism.
