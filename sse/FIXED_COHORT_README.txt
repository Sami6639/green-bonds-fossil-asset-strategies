SSE fixed green-project cohort chronology, 2019–2024

Three projects: Viking, Gordonbush Extension, Seagreen1. Selected because the same three projects exhaust the generating-project allocation table for Green Bond5; Viking and Seagreen also receive Green Bond6 allocations. This is a bounded illustrative cohort, not a representative sample or a causal test.

Delivered evidence:
- 18 project/disclosure-year snapshots, one for each project in2019–2024. These are dated annual disclosure observations, NOT uniform fiscal-year-end measurements. Some sources published after fiscal year end discuss subsequent events. evidence_date_or_period preserves this distinction.
- Nine bond/project/report-year rows, including Gordonbush's GB6 source dash as an explicit missing cell, not an established GB6 allocation link. Repeated GB5 amounts are allocation STOCKS reported in both2023 and2024, not new annual flows.
- Three unique-project records and eight dated milestones, separating investment decision, first power and full operation.
- Equinor counterparty confirmation of Triton acquisition announcement28June2022 and completion1September2022; Equinor's2022Form20-F p85 independently confirms50:50ownership. It corroborates the transaction, not the environmental impact.

Main chronology:
Gordonbush Extension: revised consentNovember2019; construction decisionJanuary2020; first powerApril2021; full operationAugust2021 per UoP; GB5 settled1August2022 withGBP37.2m allocated. Thus this observed financial link is post-commissioning refinancing.
Viking: future funding agreementMay2019; conditional FID17June2020; under construction before GB5; GBP166m GB5 plusGBP202.2m GB6 allocation stocks reported2024. FY2024 output0 through31March2024; first powerJune2024; full operations announced29August2024. Do not backfill operational status intoMarch2024.
Seagreen1: wholly owned in2019; FID3June2020 coincides with51pct sell-down toTotal, leaving SSE49pct. First powerAugust2022; GB5GBP340.7m and GB6GBP437.6m allocations; full operation17October2023 independently confirmed byTotalEnergies. EarlierApril/August2023 completion forecasts remain forecasts in snapshots. Qualifying output345.3GWh FY2023 and763GWh FY2024 reflects49pct project share, not bond attribution.

Refinancing and physical new construction are independent fields. These are physically new projects, but all the verified bond allocations are refinancing. Prior decisions precede the linked bonds. This demonstrates a traceable financial/project chronology, not that the bonds caused commissioning or fossil retirement.
